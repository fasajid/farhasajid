The short version

The problem

Beauty shelves are overwhelming. People buy products but don't know which ones work together, or in what order to use them.

My role

I designed the experience and built it end to end: layout, interactions, the AI chat, and multilingual support.

The approach

Let people pick what they already have or want, then let an AI advisor turn it into a step-by-step routine they can ask follow-up questions about.

Chapter 2 · Design & build

Applying the design

Four simple steps, from a full shelf to a routine that makes sense.

How it works

  1. Browse

    Pick a category or search for a product by name.

  2. Select

    Tap products to add them. They collect in a "Selected Products" list you can clear any time.

  3. Generate

    One button turns your picks into a personalised, step-by-step routine.

  4. Ask

    Keep chatting with the advisor to ask follow-up questions about your routine.

Key design decisions

Choose first, then chat

Instead of an empty chat box asking "How can I help?", people start by picking real products. That gives the AI something concrete to work with, and gives shoppers a clear first step.

Add your own reasoning, or anything you tried first that didn't work.

Your picks, always in view

The Selected Products list keeps every choice visible, with a "Clear all" to start over, so nobody loses track of what they've added.

Five languages, including right to left

The advisor works in English, French, Spanish, Arabic and Persian. Arabic and Persian read right to left, so the layout has to flip, not just the words.
