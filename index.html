Chapter one

From Delhi to Atlanta, and from henna to UX.

Every design I make carries a little of where I come from. Here's the path so far.

  1. Where it starts

    New Delhi, India

    Born into color, pattern and celebration. My first design teachers were the markets, fabrics and festivals around me.

  2. Growing up

    Atlanta, Georgia

    I grew up between two cultures. That taught me early that the "default user" doesn't exist, and that good design has to work for people of every background.

  3. Age 13

    I started my own henna business

    My first clients, deadlines and lessons in listening. I learned to understand what someone wants before I pick up the cone.

  4. Bridal henna

    Hours-long designs, one story at a time

    Bridal work taught me patience, precision and how to turn a person's story into a design they'll remember forever.

  5. Today

    UX / UI design

    Same love, new medium: exploring ideas, bringing them to life and solving real problems so people feel less frustration and more ease.

  6. Next stop

    Your team? ✈︎

    I'm always learning, always growing, and ready for the next adventure.

How I work

My design process is a lot like henna.

01 Research

Mixing the paste

Good henna starts long before the cone touches skin. I listen, ask questions and research who I'm designing for and what they really need.

02 Design & prototype

Applying the design

I sketch, explore, then refine line by line. Every detail has a reason, from contrast and spacing to how a button feels to tap.

03 Test & iterate

Letting the stain develop

The real result shows over time. I test with real people, learn what works, and keep improving until it feels effortless.
